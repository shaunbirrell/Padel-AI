# Phone test: trucks and Fuel Tanker (hold the phone sideways)

Only once the new built trucks ship with this change. About 5 minutes, at your own base.

1. Open the garage and spawn the **Fuel Tanker**. It looks like a fuel truck, with the round tank sitting on the truck (no gap under it). Get in: you sit inside the cab, not on the roof.
2. Drive out through your gate with the thumbstick only. While driving, the truck looks about as big on screen as the trucks do today, not tiny.
3. Tap the jump button: you land on the ground beside the truck, on the driver's side. Not inside it, not on top of it and not behind it.
4. Spawn the **Troop Transport**, then the **Armored Truck**. They look clearly different from the tanker and from each other, and you sit inside each one. From the side, the Armored Truck's gun sits on the truck, not floating above it.
5. Spawn the **Supply Truck** and the **Ammo Carrier** side by side. Can you tell them apart at a glance, from the side and from behind?
6. Ask a friend to ride along in one truck with the Ride button. Then you both tap jump: nobody ends up inside or on top of the truck.
7. If something looks wrong (you sit on the roof, a part floats, two trucks look the same, you land behind the truck), send a screenshot.
