# Phone test: floating faces and the camo blob (phone in landscape)

Use your own phone first. Then, if you can, a mid-range Android or Graphics Quality 3.

1. Join the game, go to your base and stand at the dock with your whole army near you.
2. Stand still. Drag the camera slowly all the way round you: once at the normal zoom, then once zoomed in a little.
   - **Check:** no camo cap or giant soldier fills a corner of the screen.
   - **Check:** a soldier right next to the camera vanishes as it passes him and comes back after. None stays gone.
   - **Check:** no smiley faces float on the ground or in the air.
3. Drag the camera round until you see your character's face. The marcher lines now point at the camera.
   - **Check:** no floating faces.
4. Drag the camera to your side and look along the marcher lines.
   - **Check:** each marcher has a face. From behind you only see the backs of their heads.
5. Swipe the camera fast from side to side, about half a turn each way, 5 times.
   - **Check:** no marcher or soldier flashes big across the screen.
   - **Check:** no floating faces.
6. Run with the stick for about 30 seconds, making sharp left and right turns.
   - **Check:** the marchers stay in their lines and stay visible almost all the time.
   - **Check:** nothing big flashes across the screen.
   - A soldier near the camera may blink out for a moment during a turn. Tell us if it happens so often that it bothers you.
7. Zoom in close. Run through a narrow gap or door, stop just past it and turn round.
   - **Check:** a soldier who comes right up to the camera disappears instead of filling the screen.
   - **Check:** he appears again when he steps away.
8. Turn round, run toward the camera and stop. Then drag the camera back behind you.
   - **Check:** the marchers come back within about 2 seconds.
9. Travel far from your base (or respawn), then walk back to your army.
   - **Check:** every one of your soldiers is visible.
   - **Check:** look at them from the front or the side as you walk up: each has a face.
10. If you meet another player's army up close (at the bank or in a raid), watch the corners of the screen.
    - Their soldiers are not hidden. Send a screenshot if one of them fills a corner.
11. Look at the soldier on the quay beside the sea gate.
    - He holds his rifle forward and wears the navy helmet and vest of a gate guard. This is expected.
    - Tell us if he still looks wrong to you.
12. Play for 5 minutes with your army out.
    - **Check:** the game runs as smoothly as before and the phone gets no hotter than before.
13. If you ever see a floating face, a camo blob, or a soldier who stays invisible, take a screenshot and send it to us.
