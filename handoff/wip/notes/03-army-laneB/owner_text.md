Checkpoints are in. They are switched on for your account only while you test them.

- Between your base and the Centre there is now a Forward Post: a small ring with a flag, about a third of the way, past your Home Outpost. Each one is named for its side of the map, for example "North Post", so two posts you hold never look the same.
- The Town checkpoint on your road is now a Gate you can take. Its flag sits on the booth roof.
- To take a post, stand in its ring: about 8 s for the Forward Post, and about 12 s for the Gate once its 2 guards are down.
- The guards wake when you come near, on foot or in a car. Parking on the ring does not skip them.
- Your soldiers can hold a post for you: walk with them to its flag, tap HOLD, then walk away. It takes twice as long, and a real player always beats an army. When the post is yours, tap FOLLOW to bring them back.
- Each post pays every 90 s while you hold it: about $1,000 for the Forward Post and $2,000 for the Gate (more with cash boosts). You can hold 3 at a time.
- Posts do not save. They go neutral when you leave, or after 10 minutes away. For your own Forward Post and Gate, time in your own base does not count as away.
- Nobody else sees the posts or the guards, or can take a post, yet. For them the guards are not there at all: their shots and their moves pass straight through. When you are happy, one setting turns them on for everyone.
