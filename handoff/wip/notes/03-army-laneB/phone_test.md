# Checkpoint posts: phone test (landscape, your own account)

Only your account sees the posts and their guards in this build. For other players the guards are not there at all:
they cannot see them, bump into them or be blocked by them.

1. Join on your phone in landscape. Go from your base toward the Centre. About a third of the way, past your Home
   Outpost, there is a small ring with a flag: your **Forward Post**. Its label names its side of the map (for example
   **North Post**). The Town checkpoint booth on your road has a flag on its roof: your **Gate** (for example
   **North Gate**).
2. Stand in the Forward Post ring. The top bar reads **CAPTURING NORTH POST** (your post's name). After about 8 s you
   get "North Post taken" and the flag turns your colour.
3. Open the territory list. Among the posts, only your Forward Post, your Gate and any other post you hold show, each
   with its own name.
4. Walk toward your Gate. Two guards appear, one at the booth and one by the sandbags. Stand in the ring: the bar
   reads **CLEAR GUARDS · <GATE NAME>** and does not fill.
5. Beat both guards, then stay in the ring about 12 s: "<Gate name> taken".
6. Drive in a car to another Gate (on a different Town road) and stop on its ring. The guards appear before you get
   there. The bar reads **CLEAR GUARDS** and they do not shoot you while you stay in the car.
7. Get out without shooting: now they shoot you. Get back in and drive off through the ring; they stop. (If you shot
   a guard, they keep shooting for up to 10 s.)
8. Stop about six car lengths outside a Gate ring, get out and shoot one guard. They shoot back. About 10 s after you
   stop shooting, they leave you alone.
9. Go to another base's Forward Post with your soldiers. Stop just outside its ring and wait until your soldiers stand
   around you. Walk with them to the flag in the middle, open Army and tap **HOLD** straight away. Walk about 20 steps
   back out and wait. The bar reads **ARMY · <POST NAME>** and fills at half speed. When the post is yours, tap
   **FOLLOW** to bring your soldiers back (HOLD keeps all of them where they stand).
10. Stay in your base for more than 10 minutes. Your own Forward Post and your own Gate are still yours. A post on
    another base's road that you hold goes neutral after 10 minutes away from it.
11. Check that the bar text fits, only one message shows at a time, and nothing covers the jump button or the
    thumbstick.
12. Watch the frame rate near a Gate while its guards appear. There should be no hitch or heat spike.
13. Ask a friend (another account) to join. He sees no post rings or flags, no posts in his list, and no gate guards.
    Standing in a ring does nothing for him. Then stand near a Gate so its guards are up, and ask him to:
    - walk and drive straight through the booth where your guards stand: nothing stops or bumps him;
    - shoot at you while one of your guards stands between you: his shots reach you (the guard he cannot see gives you
      no cover from him, while it still covers you from everyone and everything that can see it).
14. Leave and rejoin. Your posts are gone (neutral again) and nothing comes back.

Tell us any step that did not match, with a screenshot if you can.
