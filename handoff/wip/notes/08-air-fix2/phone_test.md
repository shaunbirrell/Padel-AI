# Phone test: jet camera zoom (hold the phone sideways)

You need one friend in the server for step 4. If you have not yet done the 13-step jet test from the last update, do
that one first.

1. Go to your Airfield, spawn the Fighter Jet and get in. The camera sits behind the whole jet.
2. Pinch out and in with two fingers. The camera moves further back and closer again, and it never goes closer than
   just behind the jet.
3. Tap the jump button to get out. Pinch in: the camera comes close to your character again, as it did before you got in.
4. Spawn the Trainer Jet and get in. Your friend holds Ride and sits behind you. Ask them to pinch zoom: it works for
   them too. After they get out, their camera zooms in close to their character again.
5. Get back in the Fighter Jet. Open the Roblox menu and reset your character. After you come back, pinch in: the
   camera comes close to your character.
6. Spawn the Rescue Helicopter and the Medevac Helicopter: they look and fly the same as before.
7. Set Graphics Quality to 3 and fly past your base: it feels as smooth as before.

Tell us the step number of anything that looks wrong, with a screenshot if you can.
