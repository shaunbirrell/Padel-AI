# Phone test: new harbour at your Dock

Use your phone, held sideways. Check it at Graphics Quality 3 if you can.

1. Join, go to your base and walk through the Dock gate to the water. Look at the building and the parked boat: a red-brick warehouse with a grey sloped roof, and a grey patrol boat with a pointed bow, a tall white cabin with windows, and a mast.
2. Walk along the concrete edge next to the boat. You should not sink in, and the yellow line should sit at the edge.
3. Walk up the small ramp onto the back of the boat, through the gap in its side rail. Nothing should stick up through the ramp or through your legs. Stand next to the white cabin: its roof should be above your head. Then walk off again.
4. Walk to the end of the warehouse nearest the Dock gate, then to the containers by the helipad. The crates, drums and containers should sit flat on the ground, with no gap under them.
5. Turn the camera all the way round by the warehouse, the crane and the lamp posts. The camera may move in close when the building is behind you. It should never end up inside a wall or lose sight of you.
6. Open Garage, pick a boat and drive it out through the sea gate using only the thumbstick. You should have a clear way out.
7. Drive toward the parked boat. Your boat should slow down and stop just before it, like at the quay edge, and never go through it. Then back away using the thumbstick.
8. Look up at the crane from the water. Its hook should hang high above you, not in your way.
9. Stand near the warehouse and the crane for a minute. Tell me if the phone gets hot or the game stutters more than at other spots.
10. Skip this if your Dock is already level 5. Otherwise upgrade it one level at a time: level 2 adds the crane, lamps, crates and drums, level 3 the tower and containers, level 4 a small rubber boat, level 5 a gold band, a beacon and chimney smoke.

Send me three screenshots: one on the concrete edge by the boat, one from behind the base looking at the warehouse, and one standing on the boat next to the cabin.
