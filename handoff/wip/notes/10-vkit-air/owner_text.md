**Aircraft: own looks, back on the helipad, and you sit inside**

- **Every aircraft has its own look.**
  - The Night Attack Helicopter is now its own design: a radar dome on the mast, a 5-blade rotor, night sensors on the nose, rocket rails and a near-black paint. Before, it was the Attack Helicopter in a different colour.
  - The Rescue, Medevac, Light Transport, Escort, Utility, Stealth and Transport helicopters each got a different cabin, tail or rotor.
  - A computer check compares every pair from behind, the way you see them while flying, and no two match. Please check with your own eyes too (phone step 4).
- **Real bombers.**
  - The Strategic Bomber is now a flat flying wing with a glass crew blister.
  - The Heavy Bomber has a glass nose, swept wings with 4 engine pods and a tall tail.
  - The Cargo, Radar and Tanker planes are longer (41 to 44).
  - Big aircraft are still limited to a 34 wingspan, because that is what fits beside your runway wall. A real bomber would be about 1.5 times bigger. If you want them bigger, the runway has to get wider.
- **Helicopters spawn on the helipad deck again, and planes start with their tail on the runway.**
  - If you have no base yet, a helicopter lands next to the pad instead of on it.
- **You sit inside, and you get out cleanly.** Every pilot and passenger sits under the glass or the roof, and the seats that sat too low (Heavy Lift, tilt-rotor and Gunship) are raised, so you do not start inside the floor when you jump out.
- **Shots hit what you see.** The whole drawn aircraft takes hits, and aircraft are bigger than the old kits, so they are easier to hit than before. A rocket's blast now reaches a player standing inside a parked plane's body.
  - Tell me if aircraft go down too fast. The fix is a settings change.
- **Camera.** While flying, aircraft look as big as before or bigger (about a quarter bigger on average). One option would make every aircraft at least 1.2 times bigger on screen, but most tails would then be cut off at the bottom. It is your call after you try it.

All designs are generic, with no real names or markings. These checks were done in a computer model, not on a phone. Please run the 8-step phone test in landscape and send a screenshot of anything that still looks wrong.
