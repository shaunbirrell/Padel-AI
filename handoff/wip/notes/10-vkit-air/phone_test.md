# Phone test: aircraft (hold the phone in landscape)

Use your own phone. If you can, also try a mid-range Android, or set Graphics Quality to 3.

1. **Helipad and runway.** At home, spawn the **Transport Helicopter**. It should sit on the helipad deck, not on the ground in front of it. Then spawn the **Fighter Jet**. Its tail should be on the runway.
2. **Sit inside.** Sit in the **Stealth Strike Jet**, then the **Scout Helicopter**. You should be under the glass, not on top. Get out, and your normal zoom comes back.
3. **Blades.** Start a helicopter: the blades turn. Get out: they stop.
4. **Tell them apart.** Park the **Attack**, **Night Attack** and **Utility** helicopters side by side. Walk behind them. Can you tell which is which?
5. **Big planes.** Fly the **Cargo Plane**, the **Strategic Bomber** and the **Heavy Bomber**. Do they look like a cargo plane, a flying wing and a bomber? Are they bigger on screen than before? Is the tail cut off at the bottom?
6. **Hands off.** Hover the **Transport Helicopter** for 10 seconds without touching the stick. Then fly the **Fighter Jet** straight and level, hands off. Does the nose sink, or does it roll to one side?
7. **Shoot it.** Ask a friend to park the **Cargo Plane**.
   - Shoot a wing tip, then the middle. Both should take hits.
   - Then stand inside the parked plane's body while a rocket hits the ground beside you. You should take splash damage.
   - Tell me if aircraft now go down too fast.
8. **Ride and jump out.** Ride as a passenger in your friend's **Heavy Lift Helicopter**. On the ground, jump out. You should not get stuck. Tell me if the game drops frames or the phone gets hot with 2 or 3 aircraft near you.
