# Phone test: ground vehicle bodies, round 2 (hold the phone sideways)

About 15 minutes on your own base. Spawn each vehicle from the Garage. For steps 9 and 12 you need a friend.

1. Spawn the **Missile Truck**. It is a six-wheel army truck: a cab in front, a missile rack on the back. Get in. You sit inside the cab, and your head is under the roof.
2. Look at the screen while you sit in it. The truck should look bigger than the old block truck did, about a quarter of the screen wide. Pinch to zoom still works, but you cannot zoom out as far while you drive. Get out: the zoom is back to normal.
3. Drive out through your main gate with the thumbstick. The gate opens before the truck reaches it. Tap the jump button to get out: you land on the ground beside the truck, not inside it.
4. Spawn the **Radar Truck** and then the **Engineering Truck**. Each must look clearly different from the Missile Truck: a turning dish, then a crane and a yellow blade.
5. Spawn the **Flame Carrier**. It is a low tracked carrier with road wheels, two fuel tanks with red bands, hoses and a short fat flame gun. Get in: you sit in the hatch, head and shoulders out.
6. Spawn the **Medium Tank**, then the **Battle Tank**. Can you tell them apart from behind? The Medium has a round sand-coloured turret. The Battle Tank is green with dark bands and a wide pointed turret.
7. Drive the Battle Tank across your base in a straight line with nobody else in it. It should go straight, without pulling to one side.
8. Spawn the **Mobile SAM** and sit in it. Your name and health bar should float above your head, not across your face or inside the vehicle.
9. With your friend: park a truck and a tank. Take turns shooting each one at the nose, at the tail and at the side. Every hit on the body should count (the health bar drops).
10. Drive slowly into a wall, then into another vehicle. The front of the new body goes into the wall a little before the vehicle stops (up to about 9 studs on the longest ones). Tell us if it looks bad.
11. Drive the biggest tank you own (the Fortress Tank if you have it) through your main gate. The gate posts may touch its sides. It must not get stuck.
12. With your friend in the **Troop Transport**: he uses Ride and sits in the back, under the canvas. He cannot shoot from there, and your shots at the canvas hit the truck. Then he moves to the front seat next to you: from there he can shoot out of the window. Then both of you get out. Nobody should end up inside the truck.
13. Play at night, or wait until it gets dark. The vehicles should still show their colours, windows and wheels, and must not turn into black blocks.
14. Spawn the **Anti-Air Truck**. If it shows a store model, that is fine. If not, it should be a six-wheel truck with a twin gun on the back.
15. Tell us which vehicle looks wrong (too small, floating, you sit on top, or two that look the same), and send a screenshot.
