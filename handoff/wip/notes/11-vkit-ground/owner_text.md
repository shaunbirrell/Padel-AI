# Ground vehicles, round 2: what changed after the review (for the owner)

Last time the new bodies still looked small on your phone, and a few things were wrong. Here is what changed.

- **Bigger on your phone.** While you drive a ground vehicle, the camera stays at a distance that suits its size. All 47 look at least as wide as the old block did. Most look about a quarter wider, and the van about twice as wide. Your character looks a little smaller, because the bigger vehicles need the camera a bit further back. You can still pinch to zoom, but not as far out while you drive. When you get out, the zoom goes back to normal.
- **Shots hit what you see.** Every solid part of a body takes the hit: the nose, the tail, the gun and the tools. Windows let a shot through, so it can hit the rider behind them. Explosions count from the body you see.
- **Covered riders are protected.** The riders under the Troop Transport's canvas and in the Armored Truck's armoured cab are safe like in an APC: shots hit the truck. In return they cannot shoot out. Riders in the other trucks' cabs, in the jeeps and in the Armored Truck's roof gun still shoot, and can be shot through the windows.
- **Tanks look like tanks.** Every tracked vehicle has road wheels, a wheel at each end of the track and a lighter track.
- **The big tanks are easy to tell apart:**
  - Medium Tank: a round sand-coloured turret.
  - Heavy Tank: a tall flat-faced turret with a sand band.
  - Battle Tank: green with dark bands and a wide pointed turret.
  - Super Heavy Tank: two guns and a tall engine house at the back.
  - Fortress Tank: pale grey with yellow bands and a second small turret at the back.
- **Other redesigns:** the Flame Carrier has a short fat flame gun with an orange tip and hoses to its fuel tanks. The Troop Transport has a rounded canvas cover. The Rocket Artillery has an armoured cab. The backup bodies of a few store-model cars were redrawn too.
- **Your name and health bar float above your head** in every vehicle, not across your face or inside the cab.
- **Lighter colours,** so at night the vehicles keep their shapes and some colour instead of turning into black blocks. This was only checked in a test picture, so please look on your phone.
- **Driving:**
  - The speed, the gate and the pads are the same as before.
  - In trucks and APCs the driver sits on the left, like in a real truck. The push now comes from under the vehicle's weight, so it drives straight.
  - In tanks and other tracked vehicles, the driver's hatch is in the middle for the same reason.
- **What still bumps is the old block.** The new body is a picture around it. On the longest vehicles (a tank gun, a dozer blade, a folded bridge) the front can go up to about 9 studs into a wall or another vehicle before it stops. A few big tanks are a little wider than your gate, so the posts may touch their sides.
- **Store models:** the Field 4x4, Armed 4x4, Scout Car, Dispatch Car, Utility Quad, Recon Buggy, Cargo Van, Patrol Truck, Escort Truck and the APC family keep their store models when those load. For them the new body is only a backup. If you pick store models for the trucks later, those will show instead of the new truck bodies.
- **No real names or markings.** There are no flags, insignia or unit numbers.
- **Off switch.** One setting turns all the new bodies off and brings back the old blocks at once.
