# Phone test: new vehicle bodies (hold the phone in landscape)

Takes about 10 minutes. Use your phone, and if you can, also a mid-range Android or Graphics Quality 3.

1. Go to your base. Open the garage and spawn the **Anti-Air Truck**.
   - It should look like a big army truck: a cab with windows, six wheels, and a twin gun on the back.
   - It should be about as long as the pickup truck.
2. Look at your avatar in the driver's seat.
   - You should sit **inside** the cab and see yourself through the windows.
   - You should not sit on the roof.
3. Drive with the thumbstick around your base for 20 seconds.
   - It should drive and steer as before.
   - It should not tip over any more than it used to.
4. Ask a friend to press **Ride**.
   - They should sit behind the gun in the back, inside the bed walls.
5. Press **Jump** to get out.
   - You should land beside the truck, not inside it.
6. Spawn the **Strike Jet**.
   - It should look like a real jet: pointed nose, swept wings, two tail fins, a clear cockpit bubble, and two glowing
     engine exhausts at the back.
   - It should be clearly bigger than the old jet.
7. Look at the cockpit. You should sit **inside** the bubble, not on top of the jet.
8. Take off: push the stick up to speed up, then hold **▲** to lift off.
   - The jet should fly nose first, and the camera should stay behind it.
   - The wings should not hit anything on the runway.
9. Drop a bomb.
   - It should fall from under the jet's belly, not from inside the jet or below the ground.
10. Land. Press **Jump** to get out. Tell us where you end up: beside the jet, on top of it, or stuck inside it.
11. Go to your dock and spawn the **Gunboat**.
    - It should be a grey boat with a pointed bow, a glass wheelhouse and a gun at the front.
    - The radar on the mast should **turn while you drive** and slow to a stop after you get out.
12. Look at your avatar at the wheel. You should stand **inside** the wheelhouse, not on its roof.
13. Look at the name label over each vehicle.
    - It should sit just above the top of the vehicle.
    - It should be small and never cover your buttons.
14. Spawn any other vehicle (for example the Military Jeep or a tank). It should look exactly as it did before.

If anything looks wrong, send a screenshot with the step number.
