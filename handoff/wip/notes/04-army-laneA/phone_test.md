# Army: guard the gate, follow when out (your phone, landscape)

Your account only; everyone else keeps today's army. Recruit at least 8 soldiers first.

1. Spawn in your base and walk to the front gate. Up to 8 soldiers stand in one line just outside it, 4 on each side of the road, facing out. The Army button on the left reads GUARD.
2. Open the Army panel. Under "ARMY" it says GUARDING BASE. Close it.
3. Walk out and stand among the soldiers. They stay in their line.
4. Walk on about 20 steps. Within about a second they start following you, and the button reads FOLLOW.
5. Walk back into your base. About 2 seconds later they walk back out through the gate to their line: RETURN, then GUARD.
6. Walk out again, through the Town between buildings and into the bank hall. Check that no soldier stays stuck on a wall for more than a moment.
7. Out in the desert, open the Army panel and tap GUARD. They walk home to their line (RETURNING, then GUARDING BASE) while you stay out.
8. Drive fast far away, then stop. Soldiers far behind wait, then show up behind you within about 10 s. None pops up in front of you.
9. At home, check that nothing blocks the gate road or the way from your garage pad to the gate, and that the gate guns still shoot.
10. Tell us if any soldier goes missing, sticks to a wall, stands inside a wall, or if the gate stays open while you are away.
11. On a small phone (or the smallest screen you have), open the Army panel at home: the cash number at the bottom-left must stay fully visible, not under the panel.
Then do the same on a mid-range Android at Graphics Quality 3 and check for stutter.
