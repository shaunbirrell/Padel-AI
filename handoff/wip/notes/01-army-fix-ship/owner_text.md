Your soldiers were never really deleted. They got left behind: stuck in doorways, too slow to keep up with you, or stranded after a drive or a respawn. Once they were far enough away, your phone stopped drawing them, so they looked gone.

What this update changes:
1. Your soldiers walk at your pace and run to catch up when they fall behind. When you stop, they stop beside you.
2. A soldier that gets stuck, falls far behind or is stranded is moved back to you. It is the same soldier, never a new one, and it is only ever removed if it dies. It lands behind you, out of your view, and runs in.
3. They are never moved while you drive fast. They are never put inside another player's base, into water, or next to another player.
4. Your gate lets your soldiers through only while they follow you through it, and it shuts once they are through. Soldiers on Hold never keep it open for raiders.
5. While you walk, your soldiers now keep their places around you and shoot from there, so they stay on your screen, the bank hall included. When you stop, they move up and fight.
6. From the last update, still on for your account: when enemies shoot at you, your soldiers shoot back, and you can see their shots.
7. In our computer tests (not a real phone yet), all 8 of your soldiers stayed alive and with you on every walk, drive, respawn and teleport. On the long walk from your Barracks through the Town and the bank hall out into the desert, at least one soldier was on your screen for all but 1 to 4 of the 101 to 126 seconds. On the game as it is now, the same walk shows no soldier at all for 121 of 126 seconds.
8. Other players get the same fix for keeping up and never vanishing. Shooting back at the bank is still only on for you.

Please run the phone test, especially the walk through the Town and the bank hall, stopping in the open, and your gate.
