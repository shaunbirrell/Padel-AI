# Phone test: your army stays with you (phone in landscape)

Do it on your own phone first. If you can, repeat steps 3 to 6 on a mid-range Android or at Graphics Quality 3.
"Steps" below means your own footsteps in the game, roughly one stud each.
A "friend" below means a friend who is **not in your clan**: your gate opens for clan-mates on purpose.

1. Join the game with a full army (8 soldiers). Your order should be **Follow**.
   - Count the soldiers around you and write the number down.
   - You see up to about 20 figures: your 8 soldiers plus up to 12 extra figures your phone draws near them. The extra ones only show within about 90 steps of you.
2. Stand inside your Barracks. Walk out of its door, then out of your base gate.
   - **Check:** nobody is left stuck in the Barracks door or in the gate.
   - **Check:** walk back into the Barracks door with the stick fully forward, then turn around. No soldier stops you or pushes you.
3. Hold the stick fully forward. Walk through the Town and down the market lane to the Central Plaza. Then keep going out of the Town into the open desert on the far side, away from the bank. Leave the bank for steps 9 and 10.
   - **Check:** your soldiers stay on screen with you almost all the time.
   - **Check:** none is stuck in a doorway, and none disappears.
   - **Check:** in a narrow lane, turn around once. No soldier stops you or pushes you.
4. In the open, walk fast, then stop.
   - **Check:** your soldiers move smoothly, with no stop-start, and nobody runs past you.
   - **Check:** within about 10 seconds the same number as in step 1 are around you.
   - When a soldier runs to catch up, its legs can look a little slow for how fast it moves. That is known. Tell us if it bothers you.
5. Get in a car and drive far away, fast. Stop and get out.
   - **Check:** while you drive, no soldier pops up next to the car.
   - **Check:** after you stop, they run in from behind you, never in front of you, within about 10 seconds.
6. Fall off the edge of the map (you are sent home), or respawn.
   - **Check:** within about 10 seconds (usually about 5) your soldiers are back with you, inside your base.
   - **Check:** they never appear in a heap, bounce around, stand inside a wall, or pop up right in front of you.
   - Turn your view around once: tell us if you ever see one appear.
7. Drive fast to a friend's base, stop right outside their wall and get out.
   - **Check:** no soldier lands inside their base, or right on top of your friend.
8. Walk into shallow water, or ride a boat.
   - **Check:** no soldier appears in the water.
   - **Check:** they come back once you are on land.
9. Walk from the Central Plaza down Bank Street toward the bank. Stop about 50 steps from the guards and stand still for 30 seconds.
   - **Check:** within about 2 seconds of the guards shooting at you, your soldiers shoot back.
   - **Check:** you see thin shot lines from your soldiers to the guards.
   - **Check:** your soldiers stay around you and fight the guards.
   - Tell us how many guards were left after 30 seconds.
   - The guards can knock you out. If they do, say so, and say how many guards were left.
10. Once all the guards are down (they stay down for at least 90 seconds), walk into the bank hall and back out, then out into the open desert. If some guards are still up, skip this step and say so.
    - **Check:** your soldiers stay on screen with you, in the hall too.
    - **Check:** nobody is stuck in the hall doorway, and none disappears.
11. Walk back home through your gate. Keep walking until you are about 25 steps past the gate, then stand still for 30 seconds.
    - **Check:** no soldier is stuck in the gateway or pushed into the wall.
    - **Check:** the gate shuts behind you and does not keep opening while you stand there.
    - If a friend can, ask them to walk about 40 steps behind you as you come home. The gate should shut before they reach it.
12. Stand about 15 steps from your gate, just far enough that it stays shut for you, for 30 seconds. Do it twice: once inside your base facing into it, and once outside facing away from the gate. Turn around once each time.
    - **Check:** once all your soldiers are through with you, the gate stays shut the whole time, with them standing behind you.
    - If a friend can, ask them to stand right at your gate and keep trying to walk in. They should stay out.
13. Stand at your gate and tap **Hold**. Then walk well inside your base.
    - **Check:** your gate closes, and stays shut while your soldiers stand there.
    - If a friend can, ask them to try to walk in. They should be blocked.
14. If a friend can, ask them to do steps 2 to 4 with their own army, on the same route as step 3.
    - **Check:** their soldiers keep up and never vanish.
    - At the bank their soldiers only shoot guards that come close to them. That is expected: shooting back is only on for your account. If they stop about 50 steps from the bank, one guard may stay back and keep shooting until they step closer.
15. If a soldier ever vanishes, pops up in front of you, or gets stuck, take a screenshot and write down where you were.
