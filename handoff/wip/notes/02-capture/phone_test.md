# Phone test: Central Plaza capture (hold the phone sideways)

0. When you publish, use "Migrate to Latest Update" (or shut down all servers), so no old server is left running. An old server still hands back saved outposts.
1. Join the game. The flag pill at the top shows **+5%** if you took your Home Outpost before (Central Plaza is not counted).
2. Go to the Central Plaza flag in the middle of the town. Its flag and name are **not** in your colour.
3. Walk onto the painted circle by the flag. A bar at the top says **CAPTURING CENTRAL PLAZA** and fills up. It can start a few steps before you reach the circle.
4. Stay there about 20 seconds. You see **Outpost taken  Empire Tax +15%** and the pill says **+15%** (+10% if you never took your Home Outpost). The bar goes away at the same moment, so only that message shows.
5. Walk a few steps past the edge of the circle, then back on: the bar says **YOURS · CENTRAL PLAZA** (full, green) for about 5 seconds.
6. Leave the game and join again. The pill is back to **+5%** and Central Plaza is not yours.
7. Walk onto the circle again: the CAPTURING bar comes back and you take it again in about 20 seconds.
8. Half take another outpost: stand on any other outpost for about 10 seconds, then drive straight to Central Plaza and stand on the circle. The bar must say **CAPTURING CENTRAL PLAZA** and fill up. It must never show the other outpost's name.
9. Start taking Central Plaza, then walk far away before it is done: the bar goes away within about a second.
10. If another player is in the game (your sister): both stand on the circle. You both see **CONTESTED · CENTRAL PLAZA** and it does not fill. Then she stands just outside the painted circle (a few steps): it still says CONTESTED for both of you. When she walks further away, your bar fills again. Two players on or right next to one circle always stop each other, friends too.
11. Stand on your Home Outpost by your base: the bar says **YOURS · HOME OUTPOST** for about 5 seconds.
12. On every step, check that the bar never covers the joystick, the jump button or the weapon buttons.
13. Tell us: (a) should YOURS stay up the whole time you stand on your circle, or are 5 seconds right? (b) Your Home Outpost comes back by itself when you join. Should it be free again after you rejoin, like Central Plaza?

Tell us the step number if anything looks different.
