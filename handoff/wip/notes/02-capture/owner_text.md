Central Plaza capture fix:

- Outposts no longer come back by themselves. When you leave the game your outposts become free, and when you join again you start without them. You take them back by standing on the circle.
- Old saves are cleaned up on the first join: the flag pill shows only what you really hold. Your Home Outpost works as before: once taken, it is yours on every join (+5%).
- Capturing itself worked: you already owned Central Plaza from your last visit, and nothing told you. We also found a real bar bug: if another outpost was half taken, the bar kept showing that one while you stood on Central Plaza. Now the bar always shows the circle you are on.
- The bar at the top now says what is going on: "CAPTURING" while you take it, "CONTESTED" when another player is close enough to stop it (you both see it), and "YOURS" for 5 seconds when you step onto a circle that is already yours.
- CAPTURING and CONTESTED can start a few steps before the painted circle and last a few steps past it; walk further away and the bar goes. YOURS shows only on the painted circle. Taking Central Plaza still takes about 20 seconds.
- When you finish taking it you see "Outpost taken" on its own, with no second message on top.
- Nobody takes a circle while two players are on it or right next to it, friends included.
- When you publish this, use "Migrate to Latest Update" so no old server keeps handing outposts back. All of it can be switched back in the settings files.
